# SeaGlobe route sources — open-license and authoritative datasets

Read-only source gathering for a historic atlas of trade and transport routes of every kind (sea; land/caravan such as Silk Road, Incense, Amber, Trans-Saharan; river and canal; road such as Roman and Inca; rail; air; pipeline). **Time span in scope: 500 BCE to the present**, including the Classical era (for example the Persian Royal Road, Roman roads and sea lanes, Indian Ocean / Periplus of the Erythraean Sea trade, and the Han Silk Road) and continuing through modern shipping lanes, rail, air and pipelines.

**No verdicts, product ideas or recommendations.** The “bundleable in an MIT repo?” column is a **license-text flag only**, not a legal opinion or clearance. Flag values used: **yes-permissive with attribution** / **no-NC** / **no-ND** / **share-alike-ODbL** / **terms-of-use-restricts** / **[UNKNOWN]**. Some rows use more than one flag when the official text states more than one condition, or when official pages disagree.

Confidence words: **we-have-a-record** = the quoted wording or figure was read on the cited official page; **not-proven** = a claim appears but is not established by an official record on a page that was read; **people-disagree** = official pages or official deposits state licenses or terms that do not match; **[UNKNOWN]** = not established from a page that could be read (including HTTP 403/404/500, timeout, bot wall, or empty/JavaScript-only extract). If a page could not be read, no substitute source was used.

Pages were read on **13 November 2026, Pacific Time (America/Los_Angeles)**, unless a page prints its own date. Official project, repository, license, help or documentation pages were preferred. Raw captures are in `raw/` next to this file and stay local. No accounts, messages, purchases, git pushes, cloud storage, or bulk dataset downloads were made. Small metadata/license files (for example a Dataverse Readme) were fetched where noted. No personal contact details are recorded here.

Additions beyond the named list are labelled **[addition]**.

# Federal tax (US Code and IRS only)

A reading aid for Title 26 and IRS notices, cited to the Office of the Law
Revision Counsel (uscode.house.gov) and, where shown, eCFR / IRS. Regulations
and notices must be labelled as such, not as the statute. State and local tax
codes are out of scope until those statutes are quoted.

`example.json` is **example data**, not official text. This is not tax advice.

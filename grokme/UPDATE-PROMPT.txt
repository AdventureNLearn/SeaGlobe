Update this SeaGlobe Lite app to the new release in the repository. Do not redesign it.

1. Read web/version.json and grokme/lite-manifest.json in the repository at the release the owner names
   (a tag or commit). Note the version.
2. Replace the app's files with exactly the files in that manifest (paths relative to web/), unchanged. Remove files
   that are no longer in the manifest only from this app's build, not from the repository.
3. Check every file's size and SHA-256 against the manifest. If any differ, stop and say which.
4. Change nothing else: no new scripts, requests, analytics, keys or secrets; keep the Content-Security-Policy,
   the credit "Built by AdventureNLearn" (link https://x.com/AdventureNLearn) and the version check as they are.
5. Open the app and confirm the footer says "Up to date · v<version>" and the console shows no errors.
   Report the old and new versions.
If anything fails, keep the previous version of the app running and report the problem.
